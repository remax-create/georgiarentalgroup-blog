Should I Rent My House or Sell It? A Canton, GA Broker's Guide

Sandra Watkins, Broker/OwnerNovember 12, 2026
Share Facebook LinkedIn X Email

I get this question from owners all the time, and I'm in a funny spot to answer it. I run a property management company, Georgia Rental Group, and I'm also an Associate Broker with RE/MAX Town & Country. So I don't have a reason to push you one way. I'd rather you make the move that's right for you.

Whether your home is in Canton, Woodstock, Holly Springs or anywhere in North Georgia, here's how I help owners decide whether to rent it out or sell it.

1. What would your house rent for?

Not what the house down the street is listed for. What homes like yours have actually leased for nearby. That number is the foundation of the whole plan, so get it from real rental comps, not a guess.

2. What would you net each month?

Take that rent and subtract your mortgage, property taxes, insurance, HOA dues and property management. What's left is your monthly cash flow.

In this example, the home nets about $225 a month before repairs and vacancy. That can be a great long-term hold, or it can be too thin for comfort. It depends on your goals and your cushion.

3. What does the house need?

Rentals take wear. A home that needs a roof, a new HVAC system or a full refresh in the next couple of years can eat a lot of rent. Be honest about the big-ticket items coming up, and remember that repairs come out of your pocket as the owner.

4. How much equity could you walk away with today?

Sometimes the best move is to take your equity and put it to work somewhere else, like your next home. A sale estimate built on real sold comps tells you what's on the table, and what you'd net after closing costs.

5. Do you want to be a landlord?

Even with a property manager handling the day-to-day, you're still the owner. Vacancies happen, tenants move out, and big repairs come up. Some owners love building long-term wealth with a rental. Others sleep better with the check in the bank. Both are good answers.

Here's what I do for owners on the fence: I run both sets of numbers side by side. A rent estimate from real rental comps, and a sale estimate from real sold comps. If selling makes more sense for you, I'll tell you. If renting does, Georgia Rental Group can manage it.

And if you're somewhere in between, like renting for a few years and selling later, we can map that out too. If you decide to rent, start with what property management costs so you know your real numbers.

Frequently asked questions

Is it better to rent or sell my house in Georgia?

It depends on what the home would rent for, what you'd net each month after your mortgage, taxes, insurance, HOA and management, what repairs it needs, and how much equity you could take out by selling. Running both sets of numbers side by side is the best way to decide.

How do I know what my house would rent for in Canton, GA?

Look at what similar homes nearby have actually leased for, not just what's listed. Georgia Rental Group will pull real rental comps for your home at no cost.

Can I rent my house now and sell it later?

Yes. Many owners rent for a few years and sell later. A broker who both manages rentals and sells homes can help you plan the timing.

What would your home rent for?

I'll pull real rental comps and walk you through what you'd likely net each month. No pressure, no obligation.
